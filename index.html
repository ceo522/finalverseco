The Process

How It Works

Three simple steps. No forms. No interviews. Just a song that honors a life well lived.

01
📰

An Obituary Is Published

We monitor published obituary pages across the entire United States daily. The moment one goes live, we begin. You don't have to do a thing.

02
🎼

A Song Is Created

Using their name, story, relationships, and personality, we write and produce a personalized memorial song within 48-72 hours. Every song is custom created.

03
💛

Your Family Listens — Free

We upload the memorial song to a private link. No signup. No credit card. Just click and hear a song made in honor of your loved one. Listening is always free.
